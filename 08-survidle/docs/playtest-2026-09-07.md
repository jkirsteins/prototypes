## Addressed since the session

Work done after the playtest, recorded here so a reader of this file knows
which findings are already answered. There is no spec document for it: the
design was settled in conversation and the reasoning lives in the commit

**Branch `survidle/map-zoom`**, two commits off main:

- `5444500` - the map's close zooms draw their own box, and the ground shows
  its grain. Closes **199** and **200**.
  - Finding 199's cause was `.grid .c` in `src/style.css` hardcoding an
    11 by 14 cell while the grid's tracks come from the zoom level, so the
    two closest rungs painted half-size ground with black gutters. It
    reproduces in Chromium, so it was never a Zen or Firefox fault - the
    engine caveat under "Bugs to reproduce" does not apply to this item.
  - Finding 200 is answered by `src/ui/ground.ts`. `terrainAt` reduces four
    continuous fields to a single letter; the map now reads them back, so
    lake and sea stop sharing a glyph, bog and meadow take their form from
    moisture, and forest takes a tone from elevation. The tone normalises to
    what is on screen rather than to the world's range - a screen covers a
    narrow slice of that range, and a fixed band flattens to one tone.
- `62dcf89` - the named places show on the map, closer than it opens at.
  Forest, outcrop, shore and heath mark their cells at the rungs below the
  default, so zooming in buys something other than size. Partly answers
  **210** (the map now carries something the HERE panel alone used to hold)
  and is the groundwork for **209/212**, which are not built: nothing on the
  map is clickable yet.

**Branch `survidle/one-do-list`**, one commit, merged to main:

- `2bda59e` - one Do list, and a row says which skill its practice is under.
  Closes **158**, **204** and **162**, and partly answers **202/203**.
  - Findings 158 and 204 were the same fault seen from both ends: mastery and
    the skill level are practice counted at two grains - 120 minutes a level
    against 15 - and nothing on screen said the smaller was a child of the
    larger. A row now reads `Woodcraft > Pine felling 1`, so the containment
    is visible beside the Skills panel's `Woodcraft 2`. The digit stays; this
    is an idle game and the number is the point.
  - The bar under the digit now fills toward what practice buys ("an extra
    stick per tree at 20") rather than toward the next level, because a
    mastery level is 0.25% speed and nothing a player can feel; the extras
    land at 20 and 50. A key with nothing left to promise shows no bar.
  - Finding 162 is answered by deletion: the advanced toggle governed a
    second, complete Do list with its own tab bar, rendered below the real
    one. It had always been there. Two lists for one job is why he could not
    tell, so there is one now.
  - Removing it stranded two tasks that lived only in that list, and both got
    a home: **haul** sits under the ground pile in the Pack panel, beside the
    take buttons acting on the same heap, and **mend** joins the Build group.
    A lean-to could decay with no repair a player would ever find.
  - Putting the mastery key on screen exposed three faults the bare number had
    been hiding, all fixed in the same commit: felling keyed on the ground
    under foot, so a chop row clicked from a meadow read "Meadow felling"
    while the intent walked to the forest; the plain ids leaked camelCase
    ("InnerBark", "IceHole", "TapSap"), which is note 158's own complaint in
    a new place; and mending had no name at all, so every mend row read
    "Mend".
  - Partly answers **202** and **203**: the breadcrumb is the category at a
    glance he asked for, in text rather than icons, and the filter box matches
    it - typing a skill's name returns every row under it. What it does not do
    is group by what an order yields, which is what "all the food gathering
    options" actually asked for; hunting, fishing and foraging remain three
    skills.

**Branch `survidle/queue-bugs`**, one commit. Spec:
[`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-07-survidle-queue-legibility-design.md`](superpowers/specs/2026-09-07-survidle-queue-legibility-design.md).
It takes finding 5 - "I don't trust the queue" [148] - as one fault seen from
five sides: the list acts and does not say what it did or why it did not.

- **Bugs 3 and 4** [128, 129, 134, 150, 152] and **finding 4** are one
  mechanism. `ordersHtml` printed `o.skipped || "waiting"`, and `o.skipped` is
  written only for a row the scheduler *refused* - so a row that could run and
  simply was not the one running showed a bare "waiting" with no cause. A new
  `waitingLine` in `src/sim/orders.ts` reads the judgement the scheduler
  already makes: `waiting its turn, behind "Gather sticks"`, or `held up by
  "<the once order that cannot run>"`, which is finding 8's complaint [242]
  said out loud for the first time. `Waiting at camp` also stopped being a
  fixed string and now reads the cell, so it cannot say "at camp" while its own
  step says "walking to camp".
  - **Bug 3's daylight theory [153, 154] is wrong, and check 1 is answered.**
    `check` never refuses work for want of light (`darkNote` states a price,
    not a reason) and `nightSkip` gates the runner only. The real mechanism:
    `orderByHand` starts a once order on the click only when no *hand* order is
    live; otherwise it queues it at `liveHand + 1` and says nothing. The second
    click lands after the first ended. It also threw away `startIntent`'s
    `false`, so a once order that could not start sat there silently; it now
    logs why.
- **Bug 6** [245], **finding 9**: `produce` (`src/sim/inventory.ts`) diverted a
  yield past `packComfortableKg` into the pile on the ground without a word.
  It now says so when that happens away from camp - the line the hunt path
  already had. **Check 14 is answered by the fix.**
- **Bug 5** [223, 224]: one camp per region is the intended rule and stays, so
  **check 13 is answered: two camps are two regions, not a bug.** The click now
  asks, and the question says where this region's camp already stands.
- **Bug 8** [140]: the order list is in `#task` at the top of the right column
  and the Do list is in `#actions` further down the *same* scrolling `.col`, so
  giving an order grows a panel above the one being clicked. The clicked
  element's position is now measured across the render and the scroll given the
  difference back. Symptom only; the cause is the layout, which is
  improvement 16.
- **Finding 7, the food chain** [254, 255, 257, 268], and improvements 12
  and 13. `VOCABULARY` in `dopanel.ts` already mapped eleven concepts onto the
  rows that serve them - `food` covers hunt, fish, cook, berries, eggs, roots,
  inner bark, seaweed, sap, snares and the drying rack - under a comment
  reading *"The words are never rendered."* The game knew the answer to "there
  are no eggs, no nothing i can click" and kept it invisible. Every row now
  shows its concepts as tags, and a tag is a button: clicking `food` filters to
  `kw:food`, an exact concept match rather than a substring one, so the tag
  cannot promise a set and then return a row that merely spells the word.
  Verified in Chrome on seed 1000010: 105 tags on the opening screen, and the
  food tag narrows 75 rows to 42, every one of which carries it.

**Bug 2 was already fixed before this branch, and here is the evidence**
[235, 236]. Two commits that landed *after* the build under test (`694a7ab`):

- `62877f3` - "a keystroke stops redrawing the game". At `694a7ab` the `input`
  listener in `src/main.ts` called `render()` on every keystroke, redrawing
  every panel on top of the `requestAnimationFrame` loop that redraws every
  frame anyway. Typing cost double a frame.
- `2bda59e` - the advanced toggle's second complete Do list is gone. Measured
  at `694a7ab`: the Do panel builds in 0.88 ms / 39633 chars with it off and
  1.20 ms / 43994 chars with it on. Note **162** is where he found the toggle;
  note **235** is where he reported the lag.

**Check 4 is answered, and nothing is accumulating.** Every panel builder was
measured against one seeded run at days 1 / 31 / 91 / 181 / 271, warmed: map
1.43 to 1.51 ms, Do 0.87 to 0.88 ms, task 0.00 to 0.35 ms, journal 0.03 ms
throughout. The log is capped at 300 (`LOG_CAP`), life events at 12 lines
(`entry`), and every listener is registered once on `document`. The "grows over
a session" half needs no leak: the per-keystroke render redrew *every* panel,
and a frame does get dearer as a run goes on - the map fills in and the Do
list's far rows move into near as skills rise, 82 rows at level 1 and 95 at
level 20.

**Bug 1 is deferred on purpose** [146, 147], and it is the one that needs
saying plainly, because it is the top-severity bug in the list. Its root cause
is that the body tier is implicit and lives in three places: `serveBody` inside
a runner intent, `bodyAsks` called between orders by `runOrders`, and a
hard-coded collapse exception in `runIntent`. None of it is on the list, so
`decideAgain`'s `setAside` and the `!st.orders.length` branch both cancel a
sleep that no order asked for. The answer settled with the author is to make
the needs **explicit orders, one per need, under a new Physiology group** -
which also answers **finding 8** (the list is too constricting: you would rank
the body against the work) and **design question 3** (auto-eat during sleep).
That is its own item, and patching `decideAgain` here would plaster over the
thing the rework has to remove.

Not addressed by any branch, and the loudest thing in the record:
**66, 70, 71, 74, 88, 140, 156, 157 and 230** - the scrolling. Note **160**
(hide what the survivor cannot do yet) is the cheap answer and now has nowhere
to hide those rows, since the advanced toggle they would have gone behind is
gone.

Not addressed, and worth stating plainly: **39, 209, 212 and 215** - clicking
the map still does nothing. The marks now exist for a click to mean something,
which was the missing half.

## Findings

### 1. The order list is unusable at its job (nine instances; contributed to a death)

[66, 70, 71, 74, 88, 140, 156/157, 230, plus 215, 217]

## Bugs to reproduce

On seed 1000010, in order of severity. These were the tester's observations
plus located code when the record was written; each now carries its verdict,
traced to the line that caused it. Seven of the eight are answered - see
"Addressed since the session" and the queue legibility spec.

1. **Clearing the queue stops a live sleep** [146, 147]. Sleep at 25%, remove a
   queue item, the bar resets; empty the list and the sleep stops. Work unrelated
   to the removed order is cancelled. *Not* explained by the daylight theory.
   **Cause found, fix deferred.** `decideAgain` in `src/sim/orders.ts` calls
   `setAside` whenever the scheduler's new choice differs from the live order,
   and `setAside` keeps nothing for a sleep; emptying the list hits
   `runOrders`'s `!st.orders.length` branch, which nulls a `wait` intent and
   takes the body-tier sleep with it. Both are the implicit body tier, and the
   answer is the Physiology rework, not a patch here.
2. **The filter lags, and the lag grows over a session** [235, 236]. "It wasn't
   THIS laggy in the beginning." **Already fixed by `62877f3` and `2bda59e`,
   both of which landed after the tested build.** The accumulation theory is
   wrong and was measured to be wrong: every panel builder is flat from day 1
   to day 271. The tested build called `render()` on every keystroke, redrawing
   every panel on top of the frame loop. Full working above.
3. **An order at `waiting` starts when clicked a second time** [134, 152]. Two
   independent reproductions (Pick lingon, Dig roots). **Fixed, and the light
   theory [153, 154] is wrong** - `check` never refuses work for want of light,
   and `nightSkip` gates the runner only. `orderByHand` (`src/sim/ladder.ts`)
   starts a once order on the click only when no *hand* order is live;
   otherwise it queues it silently and the second click, after the first ended,
   starts it. The row now names that cause.
4. **Contradictory order rows** [128, 129, 150]. "Waiting at camp; walking to
   camp" and "waiting: walking". **Fixed.** `ordersHtml` composed a fixed
   "Waiting at camp: <step>" while the wait's body tier walks; the header now
   reads the intent's actual cell.
5. **An accidental second camp** [223, 224]. **Fixed as an interface fault.**
   `campCell` is per region, so two camps are two regions and the model was
   right; the click was the problem. It now asks, and says where this region's
   camp already stands.
6. **A gather order completed having produced nothing** [245], silently.
   **Confirmed and fixed.** `produce` (`src/sim/inventory.ts`) diverts a yield

7. **Map zoom rendering** [199]: glyph grid clips right, map overflows its

   cause was a cell box that ignored the zoom level, and it reproduced in

8. **The UI jumps when an order is clicked** [140]. **Symptom fixed.** Not a

# Appendix A - the UI overhaul

A second body of work, on branch `worktree-survidle-ui-overhaul`, with a
spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-07-survidle-ui-overhaul-design.md`.
It is separate from "Addressed since the session" above, which records the
map-zoom and one-Do-list branches, and it does not repeat what those

## What the overhaul is

One rule, and everything follows from it: **left is info, middle is
interactive, right is the queue.** The HERE panel is deleted and its box
becomes a tab container - Do, Log, Pack, Journal - so the thing a player
acts through stops being the sixth panel down a scrolling column. The Do
tab carries the five groups as subtabs, and each subtab splits into the
purposes its rows serve, which is note 203 ("show me the food") made
structural. What HERE knew that was genuinely per-cell moves into a map
tooltip; what it said twice, because a Do row already said it, is cut.

## What the browser pass found that the tests did not

Three faults were on screen with every automated check green, which is
the argument for looking:

- The ways-out corner escaped its map and sat over the queue column: it
  was absolutely positioned inside an element that was not positioned. The
  DOM check saw the element and its rows and said nothing was wrong.
- All four panes painted at once. The DOM said three were hidden and was
  telling the truth - a display rule beat `[hidden]`, so they stacked.
- The Do pane showed its tabs and no rows. "Above the fold" had been read
  as "its top edge is on screen", which is a promise of a check-in rather
  than one.

## Two things this pass fixes without a line item

Neither is patched; both lose their cause.

- **The nested scroll capture** [74] and **the UI jumping on click** [140]
  were consequences of the Do list being a nested scroller in a stacked
  column with rows that expanded in place. Tabs plus a split pane remove
  that shape.

The queue - ordering, cancellation, and whether a blocked head halts the
