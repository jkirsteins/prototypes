# Playtest 2026-09-17

Preview build of `worktree-survidle-idle-layer`, desktop Safari. The
tester's own words, numbered; analysis and disposition beneath each.

[1] "if i click 'begin again' or any other slow button, there should be a
    activity indicator (and the button should disappear and not be
    clickable again). Right now slow buttons give no indication something
    is happening"
    - `begin-again` runs `advance()` over the death-to-landing gap
      synchronously (`landing.ts:158`). Nothing can paint during it. Needs
      to disable the button, show the loading bar, yield a frame, then run.
      `fresh()` already does this for world solves via `solving`.
    - FIXED: beginAgain and nextBoat run behind the loading bar, which is
      shown and painted before the work starts and covers every button.

[2] "there is no camp, yet no camp opportunity"
    - Heir in Gammeldalen. `site` completes once per world; nothing
      re-arms for a camp-less survivor. Card said "No current opportunity".
    - FIXED in the panel: a survivor with no camp is pointed at `site`.
      Ruling: heirs keep the world's board (not re-onboarded).

[3] Chip row: "remove these, i never asked to add a new button row"
    - REMOVED.

[4] "'drink' is a useless opportunity... inform people about how it works
    ... opportunities used as fyi. But the real second post-camp
    opportunity should be to make a fireplace"
    - FIXED: spec section 11. `drink` and the seasons are FYIs; the spine
      is site -> build:firePit -> firewood -> fire -> bed, roof.

[5] Safari: "This web page was reloaded because it was using significant

    - PROVEN in real Safari with scripts/safari-footprint.sh, seed 17,

        before  223 -> 299 -> 11264 -> 22528 -> 19456 MB, killed at t+50s

[8] "didn't we discuss having a toggle for fire sites? for fuel and
    keeping lit? (maintained via the camp maintenance task)"
    - What exists: the Camp maintenance row feeds a LIT fire on its own,
      a log at a time, for no minute (bodyorder.ts). It does not lay the
      first fuel and does not light: those are the fuel and light rows.
      There is no toggle.
    - OPEN, design: a fire-site setting for "light it when it goes cold"
      is the missing half; feeding is already automatic.

[9] "stamina recovery should be just 1 task with expected length to

      a long recovery reads as the same task over and over.
    - OPEN, design: one rest with a bar to the recovery threshold, and a
      higher-priority row above it pre-empting at the next minute.

[10] "why did i die of thirst instead of going to drink? is it realistic
     that it chose to sleep (exhausted) instead of going to drink?"
    - Day 2, water 0.0 l, no vessel at camp, the shore 0.1 km away. The
      queue read "Resting to 55 Stamina after a collapse" over and over:
      the collapse latch holds the survivor resting until stamina recovers,
      and the self-care drink walk sat under it. Thirst killed first.
    - OPEN, body model: a collapse that forbids a hundred-metre walk to
      water for hours is not realistic; thirst is the faster killer and a
      spent body still crawls to a stream. Drinking should outrank the
      collapse rest when water is within a short walk.

[14] "the fire is burning, why dont i have flame animations on the map?"
    - The survivor's @ took the camp cell outright, so a fire under their
      feet had no mark and no flicker. Standing at camp is where the early
      game is spent, so the fire was never seen burning.
    - FIXED: the survivor's cell carries the fire (or coals) as a class:
      the fire's ground under the @ by day, the flicker or the coals'
      breath through it at night.
    - The user's second point: the glow footprint at 300 m was zero cells
      round the fire, so nothing but the covered cell could ever flicker.
      Ruling: footprint and visibility are two things. The fire is seen
      from kilometres (already modelled, the far mark); the ground it
      lights is one cell at this rung, a weak spill on the neighbours, and
      a second, fainter ring for a large fire only. FIXED so.
