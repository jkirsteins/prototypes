# Survidle hunting economy

## Goal

Make hunting knowledge, field work and food production behave like one coherent
survival system. Hunting skill improves what the survivor can infer and recover,
not merely the final kill roll.

## Ground choice

An explicit species or map cell remains an exact player command. "Hunt anything"
chooses among reachable hunting cells using only mapped terrain, general habitat
knowledge, recent signs, local hunting pressure and the survivor's skill. Neither
the region's hidden roster nor its exact population is player knowledge.

At low skill proximity dominates. As Hunting rises, estimated usable meat per
hour increasingly dominates. The estimate includes travel, hunt duration, the
survivor's odds against each suitable species, every required carried load, and local
avoidance. Recent signs improve confidence and expire with age.

An unsuccessful attempt is evidence too. Repeated recent failures reduce the
estimated return from that cell without revealing its true population. At
Hunting 8 and above, learned poor local ground lets "Hunt anything" compare
mapped, reachable cells in neighboring regions. It does not inspect an
unmapped region or prefer one because of hidden abundance.

## Carcasses

A successful hunt creates a carcass on the kill cell. The same hunt continues
through field dressing and butchering before it counts as complete. The central
activity line names that real sub-action. Stopping preserves both the carcass and
the processing progress.

Processing time scales with recoverable meat. Hunting skill and an appropriate
edge determine the recoverable share. Recovered meat, fat, hide, fur, bone and
sinew are produced only when processing finishes. Normal delivery then hauls the
result to camp, possibly over several trips.

Carcass creation is conditional on one whole animal being claimed first. A
continuous regional abundance below one cannot be harvested as an individual.
For a represented species, an explicitly targeted wildlife subject must be the
one removed; its absence or a failed removal means no kill. A generic encounter
may materialize an unrepresented whole member, but removes that subject and one
unit of regional abundance in the same claim.

Unprocessed carcasses age in warm weather. Scavenging and spoilage reduce what
can still be recovered, eventually removing the carcass. This is state on the
cell, not an inventory item.

## Hunting pressure

Every attempt disturbs its cell and a kill disturbs it more. Pressure lowers
encounter quality locally and fades over days. It represents avoidance by mobile
large game, not extermination. The existing animal population still loses the
animal actually killed. Localized small populations can continue to deplete
through their existing population rules.

## Automated survival

Reference automation hunts toward food and fat runway. It does not contain
unrestricted species-specific forever hunts. Immediate water, warmth and food
debt outrank seasonal reserve accumulation. Winter fuel is paced against the
time remaining and cannot consume the working day while near-term food is unsafe.

## Evaluation boundary

Do not rebalance cold, secondary foods or the final skill curve until hunting
production is inside its intended band. Horizon stages become explicit fixtures;
lineage compares inherited and fresh matched starts; deep-cold reporting samples
actual qualifying exposure; food-source probes use paired constrained scenarios.

Further ecological tuning and final numeric calibration belong in the realism
roadmap after these measurements are trustworthy.

## Goal guidance

The ladder teaches the hunting loop through outcomes. "Find fresh animal sign"
completes only when real local evidence is observed. "Bring meat back to camp"
completes only when meat recovered from a carcass reaches camp. The existing
"Put food by for later" goal then teaches preservation. None requires a particular
species, weapon or route.
