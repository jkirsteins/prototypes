Radiance .hdr, 2048x1024 equirectangular, 5.7 MB. A birch forest clearing at
low golden-hour sun - matches the Latvian countryside brief (birches, warm low
light). Use as PanoramaSkyMaterial in a WorldEnvironment; works with the
gl_compatibility renderer. If lighting looks flat, add a DirectionalLight3D
aligned with the sun in the panorama (sun sits low, warm, slightly above the
horizon).
